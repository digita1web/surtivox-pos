# Prompts de generación visual — Surtivox POS

## 1. App icon (English prompt)
Create a premium minimalist app icon for “Surtivox POS”, an offline-first point-of-sale app for neighborhood shops in Colombia. Use a bold geometric letter S subtly constructed from barcode-like vertical bars, instantly recognizable at small mobile sizes. Match this exact visual direction: vivid teal emerald green #19B88E and deep near-black #0B0E12. Prefer a solid teal-green rounded-square background with the symbol in near-black, high contrast, simple silhouette, balanced negative space, flat vector-like finish, no gradients, no shadows, no mockup, no extra text, no tiny details. Centered composition, professional retail technology brand, clean edges, suitable for Android and iOS app icon.

## 2. Splash screen illustration (English prompt)
Create a welcoming modern flat-vector illustration for a mobile point-of-sale app used by Colombian neighborhood shop owners. Show a friendly contemporary Colombian shopkeeper behind a small tienda counter, using a smartphone to register a sale. Include simple shelves with everyday packaged goods and a few grocery items, arranged cleanly without readable brand names. Use a restrained palette matching the app: deep near-black #0B0E12, dark blue-gray #151C24, teal emerald #19B88E, off-white #F1F3F6, muted gray #929BA9, with a small coral accent #E66B75 only if needed. Rounded geometric shapes, crisp vector-like edges, warm and trustworthy expression, modern product illustration, strong silhouette, generous negative space around the character, vertical 9:16 composition. No words, no logos, no watermark, no photorealism, no 3D render, no UI screenshot.

## 3. Logo exploration (English prompt)
Design three distinct minimalist vector logo concepts for Surtivox POS, a point-of-sale and inventory app for neighborhood shops in Colombia. Explore a custom geometric letter S integrated with barcode rhythm; keep it bold, legible at 24 px, and recognizable in monochrome. Use #19B88E teal emerald and #0B0E12 near-black, flat shapes, clean geometry, rounded corners, intentional negative space. Show the symbol alone on a plain background, no mockup, no gradients, no extra typography, no watermark. Deliver a polished brand mark suitable for SVG recreation.
